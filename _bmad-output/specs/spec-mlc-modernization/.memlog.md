- (direction by user) Modernize Minhaaj Learning Center as a small multi-page site using only the existing Home, Programs, and About page content.
- (constraint by user) Use vanilla HTML and JavaScript with Tailwind CSS; do not introduce an application framework.
- (constraint by user) Design mobile-first because mobile is the primary traffic source.
- (direction by user) Use a modern, elegant, minimal visual language appropriate for an Islamic religious institution, centered on Kubba Khadra-inspired green and restrained gold.
- (capability by user) CAP-1: Visitors can understand MLC and reach registration immediately from an HTML/CSS hero; success is a prominent working registration action in the initial mobile viewport with no flyer image.
- (capability) CAP-2: Visitors can learn MLC mission, curriculum breadth, and community commitments on Home; success is that all load-bearing homepage text and contact details are represented in readable sections.
- (capability) CAP-3: Visitors can explore Weekend School, Knowledge Retreats, and Courses on Programs; success is that each program and the complete source topic catalog are discoverable without separate course-detail pages.
- (capability) CAP-4: Visitors can understand MLC history, religious purpose, learning approach, and intended audience on About; success is preservation of the source meaning, Arabic quotation, translation, and teaching areas.
- (capability) CAP-5: Mobile visitors can move among Home, Programs, and About and access registration or contact details; success is usable navigation at narrow widths and valid phone, email, address, and registration links.
- (decision) The existing Google Form at docs.google.com/forms/d/14PnXpYQUP97Et9Dmm6wtJTy2VGmZW26QRzHvjv-3hZg/viewform remains the primary registration destination.
- (constraint) Content scope excludes Calendar, Contact, registration, individual course catalog/detail pages, and new editorial subject matter as standalone pages.
- (assumption) Existing prose may be lightly corrected and reorganized for clarity while preserving its religious meaning and claims.
- (question) Should Tailwind be delivered through its browser/CDN build for simplicity or compiled locally for production?
- (question) Where will the finished static site be hosted and what URL structure must it preserve?
- (question) Should the displayed registration destination remain the current Google Form at launch, or will MLC provide a replacement URL?
- (note) Wrapper-only content intentionally dropped: legacy search field, duplicated desktop/mobile navigation markup, image labels, flyer image, course-detail links, calendar link, contact-page link, and repeated footer navigation.
