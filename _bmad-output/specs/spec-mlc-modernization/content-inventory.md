## Programs

### Weekend School Sessions

- Three-hour sessions once weekly on Saturday or Sunday, running September through June.
- Open to ages 5–18, with seven levels based on age and ability.
- Three core subjects:
  - Qur'an: pronunciation, tajweed, reading from the mushaf, and memorization of juzu' Amma and juzu' Tabarak.
  - Islamic Studies: creed, jurisprudence, hadith, Islamic history, and proper Islamic behavior.
  - Arabic: early letter recognition, phonetics, and vocabulary; later reading, writing, composition, and spoken standard Arabic (fushah).

### Knowledge Retreats

- Condensed workshops focused on a single subject.
- Duration ranges from a full day to three or four days on long weekends.
- Visitors are directed to current offerings and schedules when available.

### Courses

- Creed: essentials of belief expressed by the two testifications of faith, including what is befitting to ascribe to Allah, belief in Prophet Muhammad and what he conveyed, protection of belief, and—at advanced levels—the Creed of at-Tahawiyy.
- Fiqh: guided by the core pillars of Islam, including the conditions and integrals of purification, prayer, fasting, Hajj, and Zakah.
- Islamic Manners: good character, awareness of harmful behavior, and the reward associated with good manners; preserve the source hadith references and supplied meanings.
- Qur'an Recitation and Memorization: reading, memorization, and targeted pronunciation exercises; preserve the hadith meaning that among the best are those who learn and teach the Qur'an.
- Tafseer: authentic interpretation grounded in classical texts so students can understand and act on Qur'anic guidance and meanings of verses that befit Allah.
- Islamic History: events and lessons that strengthen Muslim identity and motivate success in this life and the Hereafter.
- Hadith: the Prophet Muhammad's sayings, actions, and manners as guidance for daily conduct, attentiveness of heart, and sincere intention.
- Arabic for speakers and non-speakers: a foundation for understanding religious texts, including grammar (Nahw), morphology (Sarf), and rhetoric (Balaghah), with particular emphasis on Nahw.
- Courses may be offered during the day, after school, and on weekends for children and adults; children's courses span three age ranges.
