About MLC

A clear path to beneficial knowledge.

“Minhaaj” means “clear path.” Minhaaj Learning Center was founded to teach, guide, and benefit the Muslim community.

Our purpose

Knowledge to learn, live, and share.

MLC focuses on traditional, authentic knowledge, practical skills, and the manners of acquiring knowledge. Our teaching includes Qur'aan lessons, Tajweed rules, Arabic language, and Islamic teachings.

We value face-to-face education and the lasting rapport it builds between teachers, students, families, and the wider community. Our aim is to make beneficial learning accessible and to serve Muslims of all ages.

How we teach

Rooted in tradition. Present in community.

Authentic foundations

Qur'aan, Tajweed, Arabic, and Islamic teachings provide a clear foundation for lifelong learning.

Practical learning

Knowledge is paired with useful skills and the proper manners of seeking, carrying, and sharing it.

Human connection

Face-to-face education builds trust, accessibility, and meaningful rapport between staff and community.
