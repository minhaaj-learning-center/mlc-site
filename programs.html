Programs

Weekend School Sessions

Three-hour sessions meet once weekly on Saturday or Sunday. Students ages 5–18 learn in seven levels based on age and ability.

Qur'an

Pronunciation, tajweed, reading from the mushaf, and memorization of juzu' Amma and juzu' Tabarak.

Islamic Studies

Creed, jurisprudence, hadith, Islamic history, and proper Islamic behavior.

Arabic

From letter recognition, phonetics, and vocabulary to reading, writing, composition, and spoken standard Arabic (fushah).

Courses

A complete foundation for learning and practice.

Courses may be offered during the day, after school, and on weekends for children and adults. Children's courses span three age ranges.

Creed

The essentials expressed by the two testifications of faith: what is befitting to ascribe to Allah, belief in Prophet Muhammad and what he conveyed, and protection of belief. Advanced students study the Creed of at-Tahawiyy.

Fiqh

The conditions and integrals of purification, prayer, fasting, Hajj, and Zakah—guided by the Qur'anic teaching that Allah loves those who repent and purify themselves (Al-Baqarah 2:222).

Islamic Manners

Good character, awareness of harmful behavior, and the great reward of good manners, following the Prophet's teaching that the best believers are those best in character.

Qur'aan

Recitation, memorization, and targeted pronunciation exercises, inspired by the teaching that among the best people are those who learn the Qur'an and teach it.

Tafseer

Authentic interpretation grounded in classical texts, helping students understand and act upon Qur'anic guidance: “This is a blessed Book which We have revealed to you, that they might reflect upon its verses.” (38:29)

Islamic History

Events and lessons that strengthen Muslim identity and motivate success in this life and the Hereafter.

Hadith

The Prophet Muhammad's sayings, actions, and manners as guidance for daily conduct, attentiveness of heart, and sincere intention.

Arabic

A foundation for understanding religious texts through grammar (Nahw), morphology (Sarf), and rhetoric (Balaghah), with particular emphasis on Nahw.
